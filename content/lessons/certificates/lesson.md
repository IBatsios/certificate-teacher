title: Certificate chains
---

By the end of this lesson you will have made two certificates on your own computer: a **root** that you decide to trust, and a certificate for **localhost** that the root has signed. Those two files are the pair the next lesson puts to work in your browser, behind a reverse proxy, and inside Java.

Work through the steps in order. Tick each one when it is done; your progress is saved as you go, so you can close this page and come back later.
