---
title: Putting your certificate to work
---

The last lesson ended with a complaint: your certificate is real, but nothing trusts it yet. This lesson fixes that three times over. You will put your certificate in front of a real website and see the padlock appear in your browser, look behind that website at what a **reverse proxy** actually does, and teach Java to trust your root so a Java program can connect without an error.

You need the `my-certs` folder from the last lesson, with `my-root.crt`, `localhost.crt`, and `localhost.key` still in it. Work through the steps in order, and tick each one when it is done.
