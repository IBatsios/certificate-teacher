---
title: What a container actually is
---

In the deploy lesson you were handed a tool and asked to trust it. The lesson said Docker "runs small pre-packaged programs without installing them properly", one command started a whole website, and you then wrote a file with `image:`, `ports:`, and `volumes:` in it that nothing explained. It worked, and at the time that was enough.

This lesson is the explanation. You will run a container, get inside one and look around, learn to tell an image from a container, and then go back to the file you wrote and find that you can read every line of it. It ends with cleaning up, which is the part everyone skips and later pays for.

If you have not done the certificates course, that is fine. Everything here stands on its own; only step 4 looks back at it, and it shows you the file.

You need Docker Desktop, which the first step checks. Work through the steps in order, and tick each one when it is done. Your progress is saved as you go, so you can close this page and come back later.
